# Brewing coffee

Water temperature matters for extraction. Most guides recommend water between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius. Water that is too hot extracts bitter compounds, and water that is too cool leaves the coffee sour.

A common starting ratio for pour over is 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water. Espresso uses a much finer grind and about nine bars of pressure. Cold brew steeps coarse grounds in cold water for 12 to 24 hours.
